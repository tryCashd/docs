Cashd converts loyalty points into SAR that consumers spend with participating
merchants. Consumers link their loyalty programmes into a Cashd wallet.
Merchants charge that wallet in SAR and are settled in SAR. Providers supply the
points that fund it.

## What Cashd does

| Party | Gives | Receives |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer | Loyalty points from linked programmes | SAR spending power at any Cashd merchant |
| Merchant | Goods or services | SAR settlement, net of the merchant discount |
| Provider | Points redeemable through Cashd | Settlement of redeemed value, and engagement on otherwise dormant points |

Cashd sits between the parties. A merchant never learns which programme funded a
transaction, or what a consumer holds. A provider never learns where a consumer
spends.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
