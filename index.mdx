Consumers convert loyalty points into a SAR balance held in Cashd. They spend
that balance with participating merchants. Providers are the loyalty programmes
those points come from.

## How the money moves

Two separate events, at different times.

```
TOP-UP (in advance, in the Cashd app)
  Consumer converts points ──► points are burned at the provider
                          ──► Cashd credits a SAR balance

SPEND (later, at your payment step)
  Consumer spends the SAR balance ──► you are authorised
                                  ──► Cashd settles SAR to you
```

<Note>
No loyalty programme is contacted when a payment happens. By then the points are
already burned and the consumer holds SAR. This is why a Cashd payment does not
depend on a provider being reachable.
</Note>

## Each party

| Party | Gives | Receives |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer | Loyalty points, at top-up | SAR spendable at any Cashd merchant |
| Merchant | Goods or services | SAR settlement, net of the merchant discount |
| Provider | Points burned at top-up | Release of the outstanding points liability, and engagement on otherwise dormant balances |

A merchant never learns which programme a consumer's balance came from. A
provider never learns where a consumer spends.
