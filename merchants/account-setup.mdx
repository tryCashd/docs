## What you provide

| Item | Used for |
|---|---|
| Legal entity name and commercial registration | Merchant record and agreement |
| Settlement details | Payouts |
| Business category | Rate-card treatment and reporting |
| Branch list | Outlet records |
| Technical contact | Integration and credentials |
| Operational contact | Incidents and escalation |
| Return-URL domains | Hosted checkout only. The hosts Cashd may redirect back to |

## What Cashd provides

| Item | When |
|---|---|
| Merchant account and rate card | After the agreement is executed |
| Merchant portal invitation | With the account |
| Non-production API credentials | For integration and certification |
| Production API credentials | After certification |

## Sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agreement executed">
    Commercial terms, settlement details, and integration route are agreed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account provisioned">
    Cashd creates the merchant record, applies your rate card, and registers your
    outlets. One to two working days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Portal invitation sent">
    The first owner receives an invitation email. See
    [First login](/merchants/portal-first-login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integration and certification">
    Your team builds and Cashd certifies. See
    [Choosing an integration](/merchants/choosing-an-integration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production credentials issued">
    Issued after certification passes. Go live follows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
API credentials are shown once. Cashd stores only a hash and cannot recover the
original value. Store them in a secrets manager before closing the window.
</Note>
