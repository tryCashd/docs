## What a payout contains

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gross | Total authorised transaction value in the period |
| Merchant discount | Deducted per your rate card |
| VAT | VAT on the discount portion |
| Net | Amount paid to you |
| Period | The date range the payout covers |
| Transaction count | Number of transactions aggregated |

```
net = gross − merchant discount − VAT on the discount
```

## Reconciling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Match the net to the credit received">
    The net figure is the amount Cashd pays you for the period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the period">
    Read the covered period from the payout record. Do not assume a fixed
    cadence — the cycle is set in your merchant agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match line items">
    Compare the transactions in the payout against those you recorded as
    authorised or cleared in the same period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise anything unexplained">
    Within 30 calendar days. See
    [Voids and disputes](/merchants/voids-and-disputes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to get the data

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Merchant portal | Day-to-day review |
| `GET /merchant/settlements` | Automated reconciliation. See [Settlement and reconciliation](/integration/concepts/settlement-reconciliation) |

## Common causes of a mismatch

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Your total exceeds the payout | Transactions voided after you fulfilled, or transactions that never reached a cleared state |
| The payout lists transactions you do not recognise | Your reconciliation is not recording outcomes. Check for orders stuck in a non-final state |
| Net lower than expected | Merchant discount and VAT. Compare against gross, not net |
