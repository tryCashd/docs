## What a payout contains

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gross | Total transaction value in the period |
| Discount | Merchant discount deducted |
| VAT | VAT on the discount portion |
| Net | Amount wired to your IBAN |
| Period | The date range the payout covers |
| Transaction count | Number of transactions aggregated |

Net equals gross minus discount minus VAT.

## Reconciling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Match the net to the bank credit">
    The net figure is what arrives in your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match line items to your own records">
    Compare the transactions in the payout against the transactions you recorded
    as authorised or cleared in the same period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise anything unexplained">
    Within 30 calendar days. See [Voids and disputes](/merchants/voids-and-disputes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to get the data

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Merchant portal | Day-to-day review |
| `GET /merchant/settlements` | Automated reconciliation. See [Settlement and reconciliation](/integration/concepts/settlement-reconciliation) |

## Timing

The settlement cycle and payout timing are set in your merchant agreement. Read
the covered period from each payout record rather than assuming a fixed schedule.
