## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything a cashier can do, plus manage team members, act on any outlet on the account, and view settlement reporting |
| **Cashier** | Work within a single assigned outlet |

Every team member holds exactly one role.

## Inviting a team member

Owners invite team members from the portal. An invitation is sent to the email
address you supply, and the member sets their own password when accepting. See
[First login](/merchants/portal-first-login).

If an invitation is not received or has expired, an owner can resend it. Resending
issues a fresh link and invalidates the previous one.

## Changing a role

Owners can change a team member's role. The change applies at their next request.

## Removing access

Owners can remove a team member. Removal is immediate.

<Warning>
Removing a team member does not revoke API credentials. API keys belong to the
merchant account, not to a person. If a departing team member had access to a
key, rotate it. See [Authentication](/integration/get-started/authentication).
</Warning>

## Separation of duties

Cashd recommends at least two owners on every account, so that team management
and unlocking are not blocked by one person's absence.
