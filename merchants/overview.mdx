---

Cashd is a payment method. Your customer pays you in SAR from a balance they hold
in Cashd, funded in advance by converting loyalty points from programmes such as
telco, banking, airline, and retail schemes.

## The two events are separate

| Event | When | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Top-up** | Before they reach you | In the Cashd app. The customer converts points; the points are burned; Cashd credits a SAR balance |
| **Spend** | At your payment step | The customer spends that SAR balance. No loyalty programme is involved |

By the time a customer pays you, the conversion has already happened. The
payment is a debit against a SAR balance, not a redemption of points.

## Why merchants accept it

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| Incremental spend | The balance exists only because the customer converted points that could not be spent anywhere else. It is not money that would have reached you by card |
| Pre-acquired customers | Cashd consumers arrive registered and verified. You do not sign them up, verify them, or store their details |
| Minimal data | A customer is identified by mobile number alone. No name, no national ID, no card data |
| One integration, every programme | Cashd adds loyalty programmes continuously. You accept balances funded by each new one with no change on your side |

## What the customer does at your payment step

1. Chooses **Pay with Cashd**.
2. Confirms the mobile number registered with Cashd.
3. Receives a six-digit code by SMS and enters it.
4. You are notified the payment is authorised, and you fulfil.

## What you never see

- Which loyalty programme funded the customer's balance.
- The customer's balance.
- Any customer detail beyond the mobile number you already hold.

## Getting paid

Cashd aggregates your authorised transactions into payouts and pays the
settlement IBAN registered on your merchant account, net of the merchant
discount. See [Settlements and reporting](/merchants/settlements-and-reporting).
