---

Cashd is a payment method. Your customer pays you in SAR from a Cashd wallet
funded by loyalty points they have linked from programmes such as telco, banking,
airline, and retail schemes. You are settled in SAR to your bank account.

## Why merchants accept it

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| Incremental spend | Loyalty points cannot be spent anywhere else as cash. Money spent through Cashd is money your customer could not otherwise have spent with you. |
| No customer onboarding | Cashd consumers arrive registered and verified. You do not sign them up, verify them, or store their details. |
| Minimal data | A customer is identified by mobile number alone. No name, no national ID, no card data. |
| One integration, every programme | Cashd adds loyalty programmes continuously. You accept each new one with no change on your side. |

## What the customer does

1. Chooses **Pay with Cashd** at your payment step.
2. Enters the mobile number registered with Cashd.
3. Receives a six-digit code by SMS and enters it.
4. You are notified the payment succeeded and you fulfil.

The customer does not choose which loyalty programme pays. Cashd selects it.

## What you never see

- Which loyalty programme funded a transaction.
- Your customer's wallet balance.
- Any customer detail beyond the mobile number you already hold.
