## Voiding a transaction

A void reverses a Cashd transaction and returns the value to the customer's
wallet.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Which transactions | Awaiting code, authorised, or cleared |
| Which cannot be voided | Settled, failed, expired, or already reversed |
| Window | Configured by Cashd and stated at onboarding. Requests after the window are rejected |
| Reason | Required in the portal. One of: wrong amount, customer request, duplicate charge, other |

Portal users must select a reason. The reason is recorded and visible to Cashd.

## What a void does not do

A void reverses the Cashd side only. If you have already released goods,
delivered a service, or credited store credit, recovering that is your own
process. Cashd does not recover value on your behalf.

## After settlement

Once a transaction is settled it can no longer be voided through the portal or
the API. Contact `ops@trycashd.com` with the transaction identifiers.

## Settlement discrepancies

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the payout">
    Note the payout identifier and the period it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile the line items">
    Compare the payout's transactions against your own records for the same
    period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise it">
    Email `ops@trycashd.com` with the payout identifier and the transaction
    identifiers in question, within 30 calendar days of the payout date.
  </Step>
</Steps>
