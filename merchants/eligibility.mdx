## Business requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | Registered in Saudi Arabia, with a valid commercial registration |
| Settlement IBAN | A Saudi IBAN in the registered entity's name, recorded on your merchant account and used for payouts |
| Payment point | A step where the customer is present and can receive an SMS — in store, in app, on your website, or by phone |
| Contacts | A named technical contact and a named operational contact |

## Categories

Cashd classifies merchants by category. Your category is set during account setup.

Supported categories include food and beverage, retail, health and beauty,
grocery, delivery, fuel, and entertainment. If your business does not fit an
existing category, Cashd assigns a general category.

Category affects rate-card treatment. It does not change how the integration
behaves.

## Route

| Your business | Route |
|---|---|
| Delivery platform or marketplace with its own checkout | Merchant API, embedded in your own payment flow |
| Retail, food and beverage, or services with staff at a counter | Merchant portal, or the Merchant API |
| Online store | Cashd-hosted checkout page |

See [Choosing an integration](/merchants/choosing-an-integration).

## What does not qualify

- Businesses without a Saudi commercial registration.
- Businesses that cannot present the payment step to a customer who is present,
  physically or digitally.
- Any arrangement where Cashd value would become withdrawable cash. See
  [stored value](/merchants/commercial-terms#stored-value-and-store-credit).

## Multiple locations

One merchant account covers any number of branches. Each branch is an outlet
under the same account, with shared credentials and one settlement stream. See
[Outlets](/merchants/outlets).
