## Business requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | Registered in Saudi Arabia, with a valid commercial registration |
| Bank account | A Saudi IBAN in the registered entity's name, used for settlement |
| Payment point | A step where the customer is present and can receive an SMS — in store, in app, on your website, or by phone |
| Contacts | A named technical contact and a named operational contact |

## Categories

Cashd classifies merchants by category. Your category is set during account
setup and affects nothing you control directly.

Supported categories include grocery, delivery, pharmacy, fuel, and general
retail. If your business does not fit an existing category, Cashd assigns the
general category.

## What does not qualify

- Businesses without a Saudi commercial registration.
- Businesses that cannot present the payment step to a customer who is present,
  physically or digitally.
- Any use where Cashd value would become withdrawable cash. See
  [stored value](/merchants/commercial-terms#stored-value-and-store-credit).

## Multiple locations

One merchant account covers any number of branches. Each branch is an outlet
under the same account, with shared credentials and one settlement stream. See
[Outlets](/merchants/outlets).
