---
title: "Outlets"
description: "How branches are represented on one merchant account."
---

An outlet is a branch, store, or location under your merchant account. One
account can hold any number of outlets.

## What is shared and what is not

| Shared across outlets | Held per outlet |
|---|---|
| Merchant agreement and rate | Outlet name and identity |
| API credentials | Cashier assignments |
| Settlement stream and IBAN | Transaction attribution |

## Visibility

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| Owner | All outlets on the account, and can switch between them |
| Cashier | Their assigned outlet only |

## Adding an outlet

Outlets are registered by Cashd during account setup and added on request
afterwards. Supply the outlet name and the staff who should be assigned to it.

## Reporting

Transactions are attributed to the outlet that took them. Settlement is
aggregated at the merchant account level, not per outlet.
