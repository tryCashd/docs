Your merchant agreement is the authority on all figures. This page describes how
each term works.

## Merchant discount

A percentage of the gross transaction value, deducted at settlement. The rate is
set per merchant in your agreement.

VAT applies to the discount portion only, not to the gross transaction value.

## Rate stability

The rate in force when a transaction is authorised is fixed to that transaction.
A later rate change does not reprice transactions that have already been
authorised.

## Settlement

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cycle | As agreed in your merchant agreement |
| Destination | Your registered settlement IBAN |
| Figures reported | Gross, discount, VAT, net, and the period covered |
| IBAN handling | Masked in every API response and log |

Each payout record states the period it covers. Reconcile against that period
rather than assuming a fixed cadence.

## Disputes

Raise a discrepancy within 30 calendar days of the payout date, quoting the
transaction identifiers concerned.

## Reversals

You can void a transaction within a configured window. The value returns to the
customer's Cashd wallet.

- Once a transaction is settled it cannot be voided through the API. Reversal
  after settlement is handled manually with Cashd.
- A void reverses the Cashd side only. Recovering goods, services, or credit you
  have already released is your own process.

See [Voids and disputes](/merchants/voids-and-disputes).

## Stored value and store credit

You may use Cashd to fund a wallet or store credit that you operate, subject to
one condition.

<Warning>
Your wallet must be closed-loop. The balance may be spent on your own goods and
services only. It must not be refundable to a card or bank account, withdrawable
as cash, or transferable to another person.
</Warning>

Cashd converts restricted loyalty value into spending power at a named merchant.
Value that can be withdrawn as cash falls outside Cashd's provider agreements and
regulatory permissions. This condition is a standing term and applies to later
product changes, not only at launch.

If you credit the customer the full face value, the merchant discount is a cost
of funds to you. Price it before launch.

## Fees

There is no setup or integration fee.
