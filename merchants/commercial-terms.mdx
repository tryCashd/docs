Your merchant agreement is the authority on all figures. This page describes how
each term works.

## Merchant discount

A percentage of the gross transaction value, deducted before payout. The rate is
set per merchant in your agreement and varies by merchant category.

VAT applies to the discount portion only, not to the gross transaction value.

```
net = gross − merchant discount − VAT on the discount
```

## Rate stability

The rate in force when a transaction is authorised is fixed to that transaction.
A later rate change does not reprice transactions already authorised.

## Settlement

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cycle | As agreed in your merchant agreement |
| Destination | The settlement IBAN recorded on your merchant account |
| Figures reported | Gross, merchant discount, VAT, net, transaction count, and the period covered |
| IBAN handling | Masked in every API response and log |

Each payout record states the period it covers. Reconcile against that period
rather than assuming a fixed cadence.

## Disputes

Raise a discrepancy within 30 calendar days of the payout date, quoting the
transaction identifiers concerned.

## Reversals

You can void a transaction within a configured window. The value returns to the
customer's Cashd balance.

- Once a transaction is settled it cannot be voided through the API. Reversal
  after settlement is handled manually with Cashd.
- A void reverses the Cashd side only. Recovering goods, services, or credit you
  have already released is your own process.

See [Voids and disputes](/merchants/voids-and-disputes).

## Stored value and store credit

You may use Cashd to fund a balance or store credit that you operate, subject to
one condition.

<Warning>
Your balance must be closed-loop. It may be spent on your own goods and services
only. It must not be refundable to a card or bank account, withdrawable as cash,
or transferable to another person.
</Warning>

Cashd converts loyalty value into spending power at participating merchants. The
model is spend-only: there is no path from a Cashd balance back to cash. Value
that can be withdrawn falls outside Cashd's provider agreements and regulatory
permissions. This is a standing term and applies to later product changes, not
only at launch.

If you credit the customer the full face value, the merchant discount is a cost
of funds to you. Price it before launch.

## Fees

There is no setup or integration fee.
