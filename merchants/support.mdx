## Contacts

| Subject | Contact |
|---|---|
| Integration and credentials | `integrations@trycashd.com` |
| Live incidents, settlement, disputes | `ops@trycashd.com` |

## What to include

For a transaction issue:

- The transaction identifier
- The `request_id` from the error response, if you have one
- The time, with timezone
- What you expected and what happened

For a settlement issue:

- The payout identifier and covered period
- The transaction identifiers in question

<Warning>
Never include a customer's one-time code or a full API key in a support request.
</Warning>

## Escalation

Register a named operational contact with Cashd during account setup, and keep it
current. Cashd uses it for incidents affecting your account.
