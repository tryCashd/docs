The merchant portal is at [merchant.trycashd.com](https://merchant.trycashd.com).

## Accepting an invitation

Your account is created by Cashd or by an owner on your account. You receive an
invitation email containing a single-use link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation link">
    The page shows the email address the invitation was issued to and the
    merchant name it belongs to. If either is wrong, do not continue — contact
    the person who invited you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password">
    Your password must be at least 8 characters and include an uppercase letter,
    a lowercase letter, and a digit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    You are signed in to the portal on the merchant account that invited you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invitation link is valid once. If it expires or has already been used, ask an
owner on your account to resend it.

## Signing in afterwards

Sign in with your email address and password.

If your credentials are rejected repeatedly, the account locks. An owner on your
account or Cashd support can unlock it.

## Language

The portal is available in Arabic and English, with right-to-left layout in
Arabic.
