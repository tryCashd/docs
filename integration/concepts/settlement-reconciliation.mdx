## Three levels

| Level | What you do |
|---|---|
| Per transaction | Store the Cashd transaction identifier and your idempotency key on your order record at creation |
| Continuous | Resolve every record in a non-final state by reading it, on a schedule |
| Per payout | Reconcile the payout against your own cleared transactions |

The first makes the other two possible. Store both identifiers before you send
the customer anywhere.

## Payouts

```http
GET {base}/api/v1/merchant/settlements
Authorization: Bearer tc_live_sk_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
```

Each payout record states gross, merchant discount, VAT, net, the transaction
count, and the period covered.

```
net = gross − merchant discount − VAT on the discount
```

## Reconciling a payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Match net to the credit received">
    The net figure is the amount Cashd pays you for the period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the period">
    Read the covered period from the payout record. Do not assume a fixed
    cadence — the cycle is set in your merchant agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match line items">
    Compare against transactions you recorded as `authorized` or `redeemed` in
    that period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise discrepancies">
    Within 30 calendar days of the payout date, quoting transaction identifiers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common causes of a mismatch

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Your total exceeds the payout | Transactions voided after you fulfilled, or transactions that never reached `redeemed` |
| The payout contains transactions you do not recognise | Your reconciliation is not recording outcomes — check for orders stuck in a non-final state |
| Net is lower than expected | Merchant discount and VAT. Compare against gross, not net |
