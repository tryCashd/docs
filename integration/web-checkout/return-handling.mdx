## Web

Open `checkout_url` in the browser and let Cashd redirect back to your success,
failure, or cancel URL.

## Mobile applications

Open `checkout_url` in an in-app webview or the system browser, and register an
**HTTPS universal link or app link on a domain you control** as the return URL.
That link opens your app.

<Warning>
Do not use a custom URL scheme such as `yourapp://return` as the return URL.
Custom schemes are unreliable across OS versions and in-app browsers.
</Warning>

## The redirect proves nothing

The redirect is a browser event. It can be lost:

- The customer backgrounds or force-quits the app.
- The webview is dismissed.
- The network drops between the debit and the redirect.

The wallet debit has already happened server-side in each of those cases.

<Warning>
Never fulfil, ship, or credit on the redirect alone. Always read the transaction
first. See [Resolve the outcome](/integration/web-checkout/resolve-outcome).
</Warning>

## What to do on return

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show an interim state">
    Display a neutral "confirming your payment" state. Do not tell the customer
    it succeeded or failed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the transaction">
    Call `by_reference` and act on the response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the outcome">
    Only after the read returns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Treat the cancel URL the same way. A customer who lands on cancel may still have
completed a debit in another tab or a race. The read is the authority.
