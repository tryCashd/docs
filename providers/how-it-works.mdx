## Linking

A member links your programme once, inside the Cashd app. How that link is
established is agreed with you.

| Model | Member experience |
|---|---|
| Explicit | The member authenticates against your programme as part of linking |
| Implicit | The link is derived from identity Cashd has already verified, with no separate step |
| Inert | No linking step; the programme is present without a member-initiated link |

## Conversion

Conversion is the only moment your systems are involved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The member sees what is available">
    Cashd asks your programme for the member's redeemable balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The member chooses an amount">
    In the Cashd app, in your programme's units.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your programme burns the points">
    Cashd calls your programme to lock and redeem the points in a single atomic
    operation. Your programme is the authority on whether it is permitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cashd credits SAR">
    On your confirmation, Cashd credits the member a SAR balance at the
    contracted rate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Spend

The member spends the SAR balance at any Cashd merchant, at any later time. Your
programme is not contacted, is not a dependency, and receives no notification.

If a merchant later voids a transaction, the SAR returns to the member's Cashd
balance. Points are not reinstated — they were burned at conversion, and the
member still holds their value in SAR.

## Settlement

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What is settled | Points burned through conversions in the period |
| Rate | The contracted rate in your provider agreement, which may vary by merchant category |
| Cycle | As agreed in your provider agreement |
| Source of truth | Completed conversions, reconcilable to your own burn records |

Your obligation accrues at conversion, not at spend. A converted point is settled
whether or not the member has spent the SAR yet.

## What Cashd needs from your systems

Three operations, called only on the conversion path:

| Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Balance | What can this member redeem right now? |
| Lock and redeem | Burn this amount, atomically. Confirmed or declined |
| Health | Is the programme available to serve conversions? |

Exact interfaces are specified together. See
[Requirements](/providers/requirements).
