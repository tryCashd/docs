## Linking

A member links your programme once, inside the Cashd app. Cashd verifies that
the member controls the account they are linking, using the method agreed with
you. After linking, the member's Cashd wallet reflects value backed by your
points.

Two linking models are supported. Which one applies to your programme is agreed
during onboarding.

| Model | Member experience |
|---|---|
| Explicit | The member authenticates against your programme as part of linking |
| Implicit | The link is established from identity Cashd has already verified, with no separate step |

## Redemption

When a member pays at a Cashd merchant:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cashd evaluates availability">
    Cashd asks your system what the member can redeem. Nothing is charged and no
    points move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The member authorises">
    The member confirms the payment in the Cashd flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cashd requests the redemption">
    Your system deducts the points and confirms. Cashd credits the merchant leg
    in SAR.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your system is the authority on the member's balance and on whether a redemption
is permitted. Cashd does not hold or shadow your point balances.

## Reversal

A transaction may be reversed within a defined window — for example a voided
purchase at the merchant. Cashd requests the reversal from your system and the
points return to the member.

## Settlement

Redeemed value is settled between Cashd and your programme on the cycle set in
your provider agreement.

## What Cashd needs from your side

At a high level, your systems must be able to answer four questions:

1. Does this member exist, and do they control this account?
2. How much can this member redeem right now?
3. Deduct this amount — confirmed or declined.
4. Reverse this deduction.

The exact interfaces, formats, and service levels are defined together during
onboarding. See [Requirements](/providers/requirements).
