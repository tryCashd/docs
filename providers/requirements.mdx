## Commercial

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | A registered entity able to enter a provider agreement in Saudi Arabia |
| Redemption authority | The right to permit third-party redemption of your points under your programme terms |
| Commercial terms | An agreed value for redeemed points and a settlement cycle |
| Contacts | A commercial owner and an operational escalation contact |

## Technical

Your programme must expose a service interface that Cashd can call to verify a
member, evaluate available value, deduct value, and reverse a deduction.

| Area | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Transport | HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or later |
| Authentication | Mutual TLS, or credentials issued to Cashd, as agreed |
| Availability | An agreed service level, monitored by both parties |
| Idempotency | A repeated deduction request with the same reference must not deduct twice |
| Declines | Distinguishable decline responses, so Cashd can tell a member to retry rather than fail permanently |
| Reversal | Support for reversing a completed deduction within the agreed window |

Exact endpoints, payloads, and thresholds are specified jointly during
onboarding. Cashd builds and maintains the adapter to your interface.

## Regulatory and data

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Member consent | Your programme terms must permit a member to link and redeem through Cashd |
| Personal data | Cashd passes the minimum required to identify a member. Nothing else is shared in either direction |
| Retention | Each party retains only what its own obligations require |

## Certification

Before going live, both parties run an agreed test set covering linking,
availability, deduction, decline paths, reversal, and timeout behaviour. Cashd
provides the test plan.
