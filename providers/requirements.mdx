## Commercial

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | A registered entity able to enter a provider agreement in Saudi Arabia |
| Redemption authority | The right to permit conversion of your points under your programme terms |
| Commercial terms | An agreed conversion rate and settlement cycle |
| Contacts | A commercial owner and an operational escalation contact |

## Technical

Your programme exposes a service interface Cashd calls on the conversion path
only.

| Area | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Balance retrieval | Return a member's redeemable balance |
| **Atomic lock and redeem** | Burn a stated amount in a single operation that cannot partially apply. This is the one hard requirement |
| Health | Report whether the programme can serve conversions |
| Transport | HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or later |
| Authentication | Mutual TLS, or credentials issued to Cashd, as agreed |
| Idempotency | A repeated redeem request carrying the same reference must not burn twice |
| Declines | Distinguishable decline responses, so Cashd can tell a member to retry rather than fail permanently |
| Availability | An agreed service level, monitored by both parties |

<Warning>
Atomicity on lock and redeem is the requirement most likely to need work on your
side. A two-step burn that can succeed partway leaves a member's points consumed
with no SAR credited. If your platform cannot do it natively, raise it in
discovery — it shapes the integration.
</Warning>

Cashd builds and maintains the adapter to your interface. You do not build
against Cashd.

## Reversal

Cashd does not reinstate burned points. If your programme can reverse a burn
after the fact, say so in discovery — it affects how soon converted SAR becomes
spendable.

## Regulatory and data

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Member consent | Your programme terms must permit a member to link and convert through Cashd |
| Personal data | Cashd passes the minimum needed to identify a member. Nothing else is shared in either direction |
| Retention | Each party retains only what its own obligations require |
| Isolation | Your data is never exposed to merchants or to other providers |

## Certification

Before going live, both parties run an agreed test set covering linking, balance
retrieval, conversion, decline paths, idempotent retries, and timeout behaviour.
Cashd provides the test plan.
