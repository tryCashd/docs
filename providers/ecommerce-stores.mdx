---
title: "E-commerce stores"
description: "Salla and Zid stores can become providers without a custom integration."
---

If you run a store on **Salla** or **Zid**, you can become a Cashd provider by
installing the Cashd app on your store. No interface work is required on your
side.

## How it differs

| | E-commerce store | Loyalty programme |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Install the app on your store | Joint technical onboarding |
| Point issuance | Awarded automatically on purchases in your store | Your existing earn rules |
| Build effort | None | Interface work on your side |
| Timeline | Days | Weeks |

## What happens

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the Cashd app">
    From the Salla or Zid app store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shoppers earn">
    A shopper who buys from your store is awarded Cashd points.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shoppers convert and spend">
    Those points become SAR the shopper can spend at any Cashd merchant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you install

Commercial terms are set when you install and accept the provider terms. If you
need terms specific to your store, contact Cashd first.
