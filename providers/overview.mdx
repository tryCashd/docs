A provider is a loyalty programme whose points fund Cashd wallets. Your members
link your programme in the Cashd app, and the points they hold become SAR they
can spend at any Cashd merchant.

## What joining changes for your programme

| Outcome | Detail |
|---|---|
| Redemption breadth | Your points become spendable across the whole Cashd merchant network, without you signing any of those merchants |
| Engagement on dormant points | Points that would otherwise sit unused acquire a use your members already want |
| No merchant integration work | Cashd holds the merchant relationships, the payment rails, and the settlement |
| No consumer app to build | Members redeem in the Cashd app. You do not build or maintain a redemption interface |

## What Cashd handles

- The consumer relationship at redemption: identity, authentication, and the
  redemption interface.
- The merchant network, payment acceptance, and merchant settlement.
- Selecting which linked programme funds a given transaction.

## What stays with you

- Your points, your earn rules, your programme terms.
- The system of record for member balances.
- The decision to approve or decline any individual redemption.

## Data boundaries

| Party | Does not learn |
|---|---|
| Merchants | Which programme funded a transaction, or that your programme exists on that wallet |
| You | Where a member spends, or which merchant was paid |
