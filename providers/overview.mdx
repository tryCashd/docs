A provider is a loyalty programme whose points members can convert into SAR
through Cashd. Your members link your programme in the Cashd app, convert points
when they choose to, and spend the resulting SAR balance at any Cashd merchant.

## What joining changes

| Outcome | Detail |
|---|---|
| A high-frequency redemption channel | Members redeem into everyday spending rather than a fixed catalogue |
| Liability release | Outstanding points are a balance-sheet liability until redeemed. Conversion through Cashd releases it through ordinary member activity |
| Redemption breadth without merchant deals | Your points reach the whole Cashd merchant network without you signing any of those merchants |
| No consumer interface to build | Members convert in the Cashd app. You do not build or maintain a redemption interface |
| Engagement on dormant balances | Members with balances they never intended to use acquire a reason to return |

## Where you sit in the flow

Points are burned once, at conversion. Nothing is asked of your systems when the
member later spends.

```
CONVERSION (in the Cashd app)
  Member chooses an amount ──► Cashd calls your programme
                          ──► points are burned
                          ──► Cashd credits the member a SAR balance

SPEND (later, at any Cashd merchant)
  Member spends SAR ──► your programme is not involved
```

<Note>
Your systems are called at conversion only. Merchant payments do not reach you,
do not depend on your availability, and are not visible to you.
</Note>

## What each party sees

| Party | Does not learn |
|---|---|
| You | Where a member spends, or which merchant was paid |
| Merchants | Which programme funded a balance, or that your programme is linked at all |

## What stays yours

Your points, your earn rules, your programme terms, and the member balances you
hold. Cashd does not shadow or hold your point balances.
