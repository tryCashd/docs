Provider onboarding is a joint process. Programmes differ in how members are
identified, how balances are held, and how a burn is authorised, so the
integration is specified together rather than self-served.

## What to send

Email `integrations@trycashd.com` with:

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Programme name and operating entity | To open the conversation |
| Approximate member count and outstanding point balance | To size the opportunity and the integration |
| How members are identified today | Mobile number, account number, or another identifier |
| Whether an API exists for balance retrieval and redemption | Determines the build effort |
| Whether redemption can be made atomic | The one hard technical requirement. See [Requirements](/providers/requirements) |
| Commercial contact | For terms |
| Technical contact | For the interface specification |

## What happens next

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery">
    A joint session covering your member model, redemption rules, and existing
    interfaces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commercial terms">
    Conversion rate and settlement cycle agreed and documented.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interface specification">
    Cashd and your engineers specify the three operations in
    [How it works](/providers/how-it-works).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and certify">
    Cashd builds the adapter. Both parties run the certification test set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Your programme appears in the Cashd app for members to link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
