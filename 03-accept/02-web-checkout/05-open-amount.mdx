---
title: "Open amount"
description: "Let the customer choose the amount on the Cashd page."
---

Use the open-amount variant when the customer should set the amount on the Cashd
page rather than in your interface. It suits wallet and store-credit funding where
the amount need not be fixed in advance.

## Creating the session

Set `amount_mode` to `open` and omit `amount`.

```json
{
  "amount_mode": "open",
  "reference": "topup_01J8ZP4K2N",
  "phone": "+966501234567",
  "locale": "sa-ar",
  "redirect_urls": {
    "success": "https://links.example.sa/cashd/return?ref=topup_01J8ZP4K2N",
    "failure": "https://links.example.sa/cashd/return?ref=topup_01J8ZP4K2N",
    "cancel":  "https://links.example.sa/cashd/return?ref=topup_01J8ZP4K2N"
  }
}
```

Sending both `amount_mode: "open"` and an amount is rejected, as is a fixed
session with no amount.

## What the customer does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verifies the wallet">
    The customer proves control of the wallet with a one-time code before any
    amount is entered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enters an amount">
    An amount above what is available is rejected with a prompt to try a smaller
    one. See [`amount_unavailable`](/errors/amount_unavailable).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirms">
    The wallet is debited for the amount entered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The wallet balance is never displayed and is never returned to you. The customer
learns only whether the amount they typed is available.
</Note>

## Consequences for your integration

| Difference | Handling |
|---|---|
| You do not know the amount in advance | Create your order record without a fixed value |
| The amount arrives at resolution | Read `amount` from the transaction returned by [`by_reference`](/03-accept/02-web-checkout/04-resolve-outcome) and update your record |
| The customer may enter less than you expected | Credit exactly what the transaction states |

Everything else — return handling, resolution, reconciliation, single fulfilment —
is identical to the fixed-amount flow.
